Good morning, everyone. Thank you for giving me the opportunity to be here today. I am excited to share my journey in UI/UX design and explain why I would like to join Persada Indonesia as a UI/UX Designer.

Introduction

Starting from zero, learning by doing.

My name is Marshya Salsabilla. I am an Informatics Engineering student at Institut Teknologi dan Bisnis Asia Malang, currently in my third semester. I am passionate about designing digital products that are simple, intuitive, and built around real user needs.

Let me take you back to my first days at university. During my study program's orientation, there was a UI/UX competition. To join, I had to learn UI/UX design, and honestly, I started from zero. I did not know what a user flow was, and I had never designed a wireframe. So I watched tutorials, practiced in Figma, and made many mistakes along the way.

Turning point

Glowiee won first place.

Then I created Glowiee, a beauty and skincare mobile app. And to my surprise, it won first place.

I still remember that moment. It was more than a trophy. It was the moment I realized that design can be a real skill I can build, and that a good interface can make someone's day easier. That win motivated me to keep learning and to join more competitions. And that is how UI/UX became my path.

Since that day, I have learned the full design process. I start by understanding the user, then I map the user flow, create wireframes, build prototypes, and test them. I use Figma as my main tool, and I also use Canva, Coolors, Pinterest, and Dribbble as secondary tools.

I also have basic programming skills, so I can talk with developers more easily. As a person, I am curious, creative, and adaptable. I listen carefully, and I always see feedback as an evaluation, because that is how a design gets better.
